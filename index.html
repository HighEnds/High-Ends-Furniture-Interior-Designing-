Frequently Asked Questions

Do you come to my home for measurements?
Yes! We come to your home in Kampala to take accurate measurements of your space. The measurement fee is between 50,000 - 100,000 UGX, which is deducted from your final total if you proceed with the project.
Can I choose the colors for my furniture?
Absolutely! We have a variety of color samples and finishes for you to choose from. During our on-site visit, we'll help you select the perfect colors that match your home's style and your personal taste.
What materials do you use for your furniture?
We use high-quality boards including particleboard, MDF, and plywood. We also offer various finishes including laminates, veneers, and paints. All materials are carefully selected for durability and aesthetic appeal.
How long does it take to make custom furniture?
Production timelines vary based on the complexity of the piece. A standard TV stand or dressing table typically takes 2-3 weeks, while full kitchens and wardrobes may take 4-6 weeks from design approval to completion.
What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Kampala and surrounding areas in Uganda. For larger projects, we can also accommodate clients across East Africa. Contact us to discuss your specific location.
